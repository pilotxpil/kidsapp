## English (en-US)

**App name** (27/30)

```
KidsQuest: Chores & Rewards
```

**Short description** (77/80)

```
Chores and learning become an adventure: coins, rewards, worlds and Piko star
```

**Full description**

```
KidsQuest turns chores, homework and daily routines into a real adventure. Kids complete quests, earn coins and trade them for rewards you choose. Parents approve everything with one tap.

⭐ For kids: a real game
• Piko, a 3D star who jumps around and greets them every time
• 5 worlds to choose from: Ocean, Volcano, Jungle, Space and Candy, each with its own music, sounds and look
• A daily gift: daily star, lucky wheel and treasure chest
• Celebrations for every quest, level-ups, badges and streaks
• A rewards shop with "My dream": see how close the next reward is
• Learning packs by grade, a word of the day and a daily riddle

👨‍👩‍👧 For parents: simple and calm
• Add a child, quests and rewards in a minute, with ready-made ideas
• One approval inbox: approve or reject with a tap, see the photo your child attached
• Daily, weekly and one-time quests, plus bonus coins for kindness
• Two parents manage together, each from their own phone
• Kids sign in with a family code and a secret PIN, no email needed

👑 KidsQuest Premium
One subscription for the whole family: up to 8 kids, unlimited quests and rewards, the Space and Candy worlds, your own learning packs, learning insights and no ads. Cancel anytime in Google Play.

🔒 Safe for kids
No chat, no public sharing and no ads on kids' screens. Parents create and manage kid accounts, and can delete the account and all data at any time.

Questions? support@kids.synaboard.com
```
